BIO

As early as first grade, JoAnna began piano lessons, and has since become a true multi-instrumentalist – proficient at drums, guitar, keyboards, and vocals. While developing her musical abilities, she has also continued to pursue dance studies, focusing on hip hop, ballet, modern, jazz, and tap.

JoAnna's diverse musical background allows her to create unique sounds that blend multiple genres and styles, creating an authentic and captivating experience for her audience.
